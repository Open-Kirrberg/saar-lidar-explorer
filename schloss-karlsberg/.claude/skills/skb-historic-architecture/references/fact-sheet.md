# Fact sheet: Schloss Karlsberg, summer 1789

A condensed version of research/01, 02, 05, 06, 07, 09 and 10 (state 2026-11-02). Every line carries a tag and a
pointer to the research section. Follow the pointer before you change the model; the research has the full citation.
**Tags:** D documented · D-> documented elsewhere and transferred, or derived arithmetically · I inferred · N period
norm · U unknown · HMS read from the HMS 2022 reconstruction (© Matzerath / HMS, reference only).

Ids: V = views/plans, R = reconstructions, T = texts/archives, O = surviving objects (research/01 §4, research/10 §a);
LC = Le Clerc 1790, PV = Pleine vue 1791, PL = porcelain plate, K05 = ruins map 1805, LP06 = 2006 Lageplan (research/07).
