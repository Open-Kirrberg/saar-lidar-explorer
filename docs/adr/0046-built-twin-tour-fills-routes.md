# 0046 · Built twins — fill toggle, guided tour as data, glides off the shell

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-06
- **Context:** Round 6 of the Bäckerei Heusser twin added a *Morgens* / *Feierabend* toggle, a 6-stop *Kleine Führung* and larger tap targets for small products. That made two new optional blocks in `sle-zone-catalogue-v1` (`fills`, `tour`, zone contract „Fills“ / „Tour“). A review found three weak spots: a finger-sized proxy reached a product through a thin wall, the first glide from inside the shop went through the walls to the gable, and the bread-wall stop asked for a tap in the empty *Feierabend* shop.
- **Decision:**
  - **Both blocks are optional and additive** in `sle-zone-catalogue-v1` (no new schema): apps without support show the union of all fills and no tour. Stop texts and positions are package data (`catalogue/tour.json` → `built/catalogue.json`), never UI code. The producer side checks them twice: the bakery generator strictly, `pipeline/zones.py` lightly (ids, lengths, zone frame, known targets and products, `default` a state).
  - **Pick proxies:** a proxy may lie up to 0.3 m behind the first surface only when that surface is a clickable target (a shelf bar a loaf rests on). Behind an occluder (a wall, the floor, the facade) there is no slack.
  - **Glides keep off the shell, with no new contract field.** The runtime checks each leg on the walker's nav floor: a floor step over 0.45 m (the shop floor is 0.7 m above the street) or more than 3 m without floor counts as a wall. The authored `via` is used when it is clear. Otherwise the shortest chain of clear legs over the tour's own points (stop eyes and `via`) is used. If there is none, the glide goes straight as before. The bakery's counter stop names the door (foot of the steps → just inside) as its `via`, so every way in or out of the shop uses the door.
  - **A product stop needs a filled shop:** when the tour reaches a stop whose target holds product slots and the current fill places none of them, it switches to the default fill and shows the usual toast. Stop copy addresses mouse and touch („Klick oder tipp …“).
- **Consequences:** Tours stay plain data and old apps are unaffected. A route's quality depends on the tour's points: a new shop needs a door `via` on one stop. Long routes (inside → gable ≈ 40 m) still take at most 3 s, so they feel quick.
