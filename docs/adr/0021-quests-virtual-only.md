# 0021 · 🧭 Quests stay virtual until a heritage partner agrees

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-04
- **Origin:** v2 workshop

## Context
The core is "exploring your home like a game; finding relics like a profession". Relics are the MVP of real actions on real land. A game that sends people onto monuments, Natura 2000 areas or UXO sites would be harmful and illegal.

## Decision
- **v2:** simple relict finding only (lens, glow, *Merken*). No quests, no professions.
- **v3:** quests and professions, **virtual only**: no "go there" prompts, no own position next to candidates.
- *Mein Standort*, on-site quests and F18 outside Labor stay off until a heritage partner (LDA Saarland, Heimatverein Kirrberg, Saarforst) agrees (PRD open question Q-32).

## Consequences
- ✅ Gamification without field guidance.
- ⏳ v3's on-site part depends on Q-32, which nobody owns yet.
