# 0047 · Bäckerei Heusser — Abholschrank and Backstube entrance in scope

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-06
- **Context:** On 2026-11-06 the user asked for three changes to the twin: both shop windows the same size, the *Abholschrank* (pickup cabinet) beside the main entrance, and the Backstube entrance (the steps at the left end of the street facade). The cabinet stands in the covered passage beside the porch. [ADR 0043](0043-built-twin-package-reference-nav.md) kept that passage public only up to the shop's side opening (frame B `y ≤ 1.60`), and [ADR 0044](0044-baeckerei-heusser-owner-consent.md) therefore left the cabinet unbuilt. The cabinet and the Backstube entrance are part of the shop and its exterior, which the owners' consent (ADR 0044) covers. The residents' part of the passage is not covered.
- **Decision:**
  - **Passage privacy line 1.60 → 4.35** (frame B y), just behind the cabinet. This supersedes the line in ADR 0043. `plan/scripts/redact.py` and `plan/check_plan.py` hold the value, and a test checks that both are the same. The public plan carries the cabinet (site fixture X1) with its position. DGM sample points inside the passage stay out of the public plan, even in front of the line. Everything further in stays out of the model, the nav floor and every public file: the residents' door, the mailboxes, the notice board, the courtyard. The public passage ends at a plain wall on the line.
  - **Abholschrank:** built at its true place and size (W 0.90 ± 0.10, D 0.42 ± 0.05, H 1.75 ± 0.10 from the passage floor; sources: mesh, video, photo), with closed louvred doors and the black handle band. Its sign shows only the heading „Abholstation“. The paper sheet's text, QR code, bank details and stickers are never reproduced.
  - **Backstube entrance:** the recessed niche at the SSW end of the street facade, with 5 risers of terrazzo steps, a round-bar rail and glass blocks on the right, is the **entrance to the Backstube** (user, 2026-11-06). An older private note called it a house entrance; this decision re-classifies it. The twin shows the outside only: the aluminium door stays closed, nothing behind it is modelled, and the steps are walkable up to the door.
  - **Shop windows equal:** O1 = O2 = 1.905 ± 0.15 m (the Street View glass ratio). This replaces the mesh's 1.70 / 2.11, whose O1 reveal was soft. The outer reveals stay where they were, and pier 1 moves. Measurement A in the *Messliste* is now 5.645 m.
  - **Keystones** are trapezoids (wider at the top). Keystone 2 reads „H.H.“ in Fraktur over „AD 1933“ (the shop's lettering, ADR 0044).
- **Consequences:** The catalogue rows `fassade/abholschrank` and `fassade/backstube-eingang` are built, clickable and discoverable, so the curated objects go from 19 to 21. The passage gets red-brown herringbone paving and a brown plinth band. Slot drains are not shown because their positions are unknown. The private facts file no longer lists the cabinet. The capture's privacy crop is unchanged (it is reference only). `bh-datenschutz` reviews now check the 4.35 line: nothing beyond it, and no mailboxes, door or names.
